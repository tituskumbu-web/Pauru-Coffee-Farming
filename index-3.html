Our Estate

Rooted in the Highlands

Origin Peak is a family-operated coffee farm dedicated to regenerative agriculture and transparent sourcing. We grow Arabica on volcanic soils under shade canopies that protect biodiversity and build long-term resilience.

Our work begins long before the harvest: selecting resilient varieties, nurturing living soil, and partnering with neighboring smallholders who share our standards for quality and environmental care.

From Seed to Cup

The Journey of Our Coffee

Every stage is intentional — from nursery to drying patio — so the final cup reflects the place it was grown.

01

Nursery & Planting

Seeds from selected mother trees are raised in shaded nurseries. Seedlings are transplanted at the right altitude and soil conditions for optimal development.

02

Shade-Grown Cultivation

Coffee thrives under a diverse canopy of native trees. Shade moderates temperature, reduces water stress, and supports birds and beneficial insects.

03

Selective Harvest

Only ripe cherries are hand-picked in multiple passes. Selective harvesting preserves quality and ensures consistent sugar and flavor development.

04

Processing & Drying

Cherries are processed using washed, natural, honey, or experimental methods. Careful drying on raised beds locks in clarity and complexity.

Knowledge

About Coffee Farming

Coffee farming is both agriculture and craft. Understanding the plant, climate, and post-harvest work is essential to producing specialty-grade beans.

The Coffee Plant

Coffea arabica prefers elevations above 1,000 m, mild temperatures, and well-drained volcanic or loamy soils. It takes 3–4 years for a tree to produce its first commercial crop.

  • Arabica vs. Robusta varieties
  • Flowering and cherry development
  • Pruning for health and yield

Climate & Terroir

Altitude, rainfall patterns, temperature swings, and soil chemistry shape acidity, body, and aromatic compounds. Highland microclimates often produce the most distinctive cups.

  • Elevation and maturation speed
  • Shade vs. full-sun systems
  • Water management & drought resilience

Regenerative Practices

Healthy farms build soil organic matter, protect watersheds, and maintain biodiversity. Compost, cover crops, and integrated pest management reduce reliance on external inputs.

  • Living soil & composting
  • Biodiversity corridors
  • Carbon and water stewardship
